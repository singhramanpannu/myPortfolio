About Me

I’m a full-stack engineer focused on building clean, performant web experiences with a people-first mindset. Over the past 2.5 years, I’ve worked across AEM, React, JavaScript/TypeScript, and Java—shipping reliable UI, composable components, and integrations that scale.

I enjoy shaping front-end systems, streamlining developer workflows, and closing the loop with automation. My approach is pragmatic: accessibility by default, thoughtful interactions, and simple solutions that are easy to maintain.

Recently, I’ve contributed to AEM component libraries, React feature work, and automation that improves CI signal and developer velocity. I’m always learning and I care about details—from empty states to error paths.

  • • Strengths: AEM, React, JS/TS, Java, Groovy, HTML/CSS
  • • Also: accessibility, performance, REST APIs, automation
